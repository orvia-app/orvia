# Orvia Visual and Product References

**Status:** RESEARCH EVIDENCE, not Orvia requirements by itself
**Research date:** 1 October 2026
**Source policy:** official/primary sources only in the matrix below

External products provide evidence about design choices and process. They do not define Orvia’s brand, information architecture, token values, or component appearance.

## Reference matrix

| [Raycast: 2026 redesign](https://www.raycast.com/blog/the-new-raycast) | Raycast describes a fresh but familiar platform-aligned interface, a shared AI composer, settings reorganization, foundational rewrite, and openly lists beta gaps. | Preserve learned mechanics during redesign and state incomplete beta scope honestly. | Familiar interaction, coherent shared surfaces, and explicit CURRENT/OPEN status. | Liquid Glass, macOS-specific visual treatment, or Raycast's AI model/product scope. |

| [Atlassian Design Foundations](https://atlassian.design/foundations) | Atlassian exposes foundations for accessibility, content, spacing, grid, color, typography, iconography, elevation, borders, and radius. | Maintain named foundation categories rather than page-local styling. | The semantic token and cross-cutting document structure. | Atlassian component visuals, enterprise density, or brand tokens. |

## Synthesis for Orvia

The useful common thread is disciplined coherence: make the core job visually obvious, reduce supporting chrome, define a small reusable language, test real states across the product, and be candid about gaps. The sources do not justify copying dark palettes, fonts, glass, gradients, command launchers, tab metaphors, navigation layouts, or AI experiences.

## Research limits

This review is based on published official material and product documentation, not exhaustive hands-on audits of every product/version. “Observed fact” refers to what a source explicitly presents. Orvia adoption remains a separate product decision recorded in its own specifications.
